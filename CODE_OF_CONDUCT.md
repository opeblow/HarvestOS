# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in HarvestOS welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and considerate in discussion and review.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Respect different perspectives and lived experiences.
- Protect farmers and partners: never publish personal data, credentials, or private conversation content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security contact. Include enough context to investigate; reports will be handled discreetly.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
